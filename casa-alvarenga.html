The House

Restored with patience, lived in with care.

Built in 1867 as a family farmhouse, Casa Alvarenga was restored over three years by local craftspeople. The stone, timber and light are original. What we added is only what a good night's sleep requires.

Days here follow the mountain: slow breakfasts by the river, long walks to the falls, a fire lit before dinner. There is no reception queue and no television, just a team that knows your name.
