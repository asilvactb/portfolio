Commentary

What happened

Revenue

September revenue of €547k beat budget by 3.2%, driven by two large repeat orders in industrial fittings.

Margin

Gross margin rose to 44.1% from 40.0% in January, ahead of the 43% assumed in the budget, as sales grew faster than cost of sales. Year to date, cost of sales is 2.9% over budget, which explains the €30k EBITDA gap. Raw-material pricing is the risk to watch in Q4.

Cash

Cash closed at €1.22m, up €94k on August, supported by EBITDA of €104k in the month and steady collections.

Recommendations

Three actions for Q4

1. Close the profit gapKeep September's 44% gross margin as the baseline for new quotes; that pace closes the €30k YTD gap in about a quarter.
2. Renegotiate inputsConsolidate the two largest raw-material suppliers before the October price review.
3. Put cash to workKeep a buffer of three months of outflows and move the surplus into a short-term deposit.
